// Project 01

Traffic Intelligence

Real-time adaptive traffic signal for a 4-way chowk intersection. Pakistan's standard fixed 120s timer wastes green time on empty roads. YOLOv5 detects vehicles per lane. A Greedy scheduler allocates green time based on live congestion — cars queue at the stop line on red and release when it's their turn.

// What I Learned

Impact: Simulated a 31% reduction in average wait time. The greedy scheduler proved more effective than fixed-timer systems, but required careful tuning of the max-heap to avoid starvation on low-traffic lanes.

// What I Learned

Tradeoff: SMOTE outperformed class-weighting, but generated synthetic samples that occasionally overlapped with legitimate patterns. We resolved this by applying a stricter threshold (0.6) for fraud classification, increasing precision without sacrificing recall.
